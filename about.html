About

Written by people who carry their bags, not collect them.

I.

What this journal is

Handbag Edit is a small, independent publication about leather handbags as useful objects. We write about shape, size, construction and care, because those are the things that decide whether a bag earns its place on your arm.

II.

What it is not

We do not sell bags, rank brands or publish sponsored recommendations. We do not offer authentication or valuations, and we do not claim that any bag will hold or gain value. Our interest ends at whether it is well made and right for you.

III.

How the guides are made

Each guide is drafted from hands-on experience, compared with published guidance from tanneries and leather-care specialists, and reviewed by a second reader. When advice differs by leather type, we say so, and we tell you when a specialist is the better answer.

IV.

How to hold us to it

If you find an error, or a guide that leaves a question unanswered, please write. Corrections are made promptly and noted on the page. Our full approach is set out in the Editorial Policy.

Handbag Edit is based at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
